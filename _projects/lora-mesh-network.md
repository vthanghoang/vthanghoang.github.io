## Overview

Studied routing strategies for low-power wireless sensor networks and implemented a LoRa Mesh topology for reliable long-range communication.

## Results

- Extended effective communication range by **71%**, from 350 m to 600 m.
- Maintained data reliability while introducing multi-hop communication.
- Evaluated system-level power strategies supporting up to **300 days** of continuous node operation with integrated energy sources.
- Received the **Best Presentation Award at Student Forum 2023** {% cite tran2023lora %}.
