## Overview

This research investigates light-tissue interaction in human skin using a multi-layered Monte Carlo simulation framework. The goal is to construct a diffuse spectral reflectance database that can support AI-driven diagnostic models when real clinical data are limited or expensive to collect.

## Contributions

- Built a simulation workflow for generating spectral reflectance data from layered skin models.
- Validated the simulated dataset against real skin measurements, achieving **97% agreement**.
- Established a repeatable synthetic-data pipeline for downstream diagnostic model training.
- Contributed to two publications on database generation and real-measurement validation {% cite tran2025database ho2025validation %}.
