## Overview

Facilitated a cross-functional team through problem definition, prototyping, testing, and iteration for a community water-purification challenge in the GLOBAL EPICS programme at Arizona State University.

The team delivered a deployable prototype designed around local requirements and real-world technical and resource constraints. The project received the **First Prize Award**.
