## Overview

Proposed and designed a LoRaWAN-based IoT system for monitoring flood-water levels in urban areas of Da Nang, Vietnam.

The system design was presented as **“IoT Flood-Water Measurement System for Urban Areas in Vietnam”** at IEEE ICCITX.O & SHelC 2024. It placed third among teams representing France, Morocco, Canada, and Vietnam.
