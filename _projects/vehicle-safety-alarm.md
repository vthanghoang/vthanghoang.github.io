## Overview

Developed a connected embedded monitoring system intended to detect an occupant left in a vehicle and send a location-aware alert.

## System

- **Controller:** ESP32
- **Sensing:** SHT41 environmental sensing and HLK-LD2410 presence detection
- **Connectivity:** GPS localization and Firebase-based web interface

The prototype achieved **95% occupant-detection accuracy** and end-to-end alert latency of **under 40 seconds**.
