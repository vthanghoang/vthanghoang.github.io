## Overview

As part of LYDINC's international education and STEM outreach activities, I supported the organizing team at **KLESF 2026 in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia**. I served as a **Facilitator** for the **AI-Generated Content Competition (AIGC)** and **Engineering Design for Community (ED4C)**, helping the competitions run smoothly while supporting students during their preparation and presentation sessions.

This experience connected Vietnamese learners with the wider ASEAN STEM community and created a practical pathway from local innovation activities to international exchange.

KLESF is an international STEM platform designed to promote science, technology, engineering and mathematics among school students. The 2026 programme provided an opportunity for participants to present projects, exchange ideas and engage with educators, engineers and researchers from across the region.

## AIGC session: GenAI mentoring and student presentation

For the AIGC competition, I supported and guided students from **iSchool** in using **Generative AI** and **Prompt Engineering** to develop creative outputs around **ASEAN relationships**. The mentoring process focused on turning a broad theme into a clear visual concept, writing effective prompts, refining the generated content and preparing a concise presentation for the judging session.

The students developed posters, stories and images that combined cultural communication with AI-assisted creativity. My role was to help them structure their ideas, improve prompt quality, iterate on the outputs and present the final concept confidently.

The supported teams achieved the following results:

- **01 Gold Award**
- **04 Silver Awards**
- **02 Bronze Awards**

These results demonstrate how structured mentoring, iterative prompting and clear communication can help students transform an open-ended theme into a presentable AI-assisted product.

## ED4C session: facilitation of engineering design presentations

ED4C (**Engineering Design for Community**) focuses on using engineering design to respond to real community needs. For this competition, I worked as a Facilitator for the organizing team, supporting different presentation rounds and helping maintain a consistent, clear and efficient session flow.

My responsibilities included coordinating transitions between teams, supporting presenters and judges, checking that each round followed the intended schedule, and helping resolve practical issues during the event. This role required close attention to timing, communication and the needs of both students and the organizing committee.

## Outcomes and learning

This international assignment strengthened my experience in:

- facilitating multi-team STEM competitions in an international setting;
- mentoring students in Generative AI, prompt design and visual storytelling;
- coordinating presentation schedules, judging sessions and participant support;
- communicating across different educational and cultural contexts;
- connecting hands-on engineering design with community-oriented problem solving.

The experience also reinforced the value of a continuous development pathway: students can move from local STEM recognition to regional exposure, improve their products through mentoring and communicate their ideas to a broader international audience.
